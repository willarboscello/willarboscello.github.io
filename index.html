About Me

My name is Will Arboscello and I am from Greenville, South Carolina. I currently go to UNC Charlotte and I am majoring in Sports Data Analytics. I have always been a big sports fan and spend a lot of my free time watching sports. Basketball is probably the sport I am most interested in, but I also like following football, baseball, and other sports.

One of the main reasons I chose Sports Data Analytics is because I have always liked looking at sports stats. I like comparing players and teams and seeing what the numbers can tell you. I think it is interesting that there can be a lot more going on in a game than what you can see just by watching it.

Since being at UNC Charlotte, I have started learning more about working with data. I have been learning how to use Python and different tools to organize data, make graphs, and find different patterns. I am still pretty new to a lot of it, so there are definitely things I need to get better at. I think the more projects I do and the more I practice, the easier it will get.

In the future, I would like to work somewhere in sports, especially with basketball or sports data. While I am in college, I want to get more experience, get better at using Python, and learn more about how sports teams actually use analytics. My main goal is to keep improving my skills and hopefully be able to turn something I already enjoy into a career.
